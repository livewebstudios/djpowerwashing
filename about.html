About D&J Power Washing — Saddle Brook, Bergen County NJ

A family-owned and operated exterior cleaning company built on three words: Precision. Care. Excellence.

More Than a Power Washing Company

Welcome to D&J Power Washing LLC & More — where we're not just cleaning your property, we're protecting it, restoring it, and taking personal pride in how it looks when we leave.

We are a family-owned and operated exterior cleaning company based in Saddle Brook, New Jersey. John and Danielle built this business from the ground up, and they're still the driving force behind everything we do. When you hire D&J, you're not getting a franchise or a call center — you're getting people who live in Bergen County, care about their reputation, and stand behind every single job.

Meet John & Danielle

John — Owner & Lead Technician

John is the hands-on heart of D&J Power Washing. With years of experience in professional exterior cleaning, John leads every major project with a commitment to quality that's visible in the results. His approach is simple: do the job right, use the right products, and don't leave until the customer is happy.

Danielle — Owner & Operations

Danielle keeps D&J running smoothly — from scheduling and customer communication to ensuring every client experience reflects the company's promise of precision, care, and excellence. Her attention to detail and dedication to customer satisfaction are what turn first-time clients into long-term relationships.
