D&J Power Washing — Serving All of Bergen County, NJ

D&J Power Washing LLC & More is a family-owned exterior cleaning company headquartered in Saddle Brook, in the heart of Bergen County, New Jersey. Owners John and Danielle have built the business one driveway, one house wash, and one commercial building at a time — and today D&J serves homeowners and business owners across all 70+ municipalities of Bergen County. From the dense neighborhoods near the Hackensack River to the quieter residential streets of Paramus, Fair Lawn, and Saddle Brook, our service area is wherever Bergen County extends.

Bergen County is one of the most densely populated and architecturally diverse counties in New Jersey. We work on classic post-war ranches, century-old colonials, modern townhouses, multi-family rentals, mixed-use storefronts, restaurants, schools, and warehouses. The North Jersey climate — humid summers, snowy winters, abundant tree cover — is exceptionally tough on exteriors, which is why a regular professional cleaning isn't a luxury here. It's basic property maintenance.

Whether your property is in a corner of Bergen County we visit every week or a town we haven't been to recently, the answer is the same: D&J Power Washing covers it. Call us, describe the work, and we'll schedule a free on-site estimate at your convenience.

Our Power Washing Services Across Bergen County, NJ

Soft House Wash

Low-pressure soft washing safely removes algae, mold, mildew, and oxidation from stucco, stone, vinyl, and painted siding throughout Bergen County, NJ. The right technique for any home exterior.

Driveway, Patio & Sidewalk Cleaning

High-powered pressure washing lifts oil stains, tire marks, algae, and ground-in grime from concrete, asphalt, and pavers across Bergen County properties. Your hardscape will look new again.

Deck & Paver Cleaning

We restore weathered wood decks and tired brick pavers throughout Bergen County, removing algae, moss, and UV oxidation, then re-sanding paver joints for a polished finish.

Gutter Cleaning

Clogged gutters cause water damage, foundation problems, and erosion. We clear leaves and debris and restore proper drainage on Bergen County homes year-round.

Commercial Building Wash

Tailored pressure washing for retail storefronts, offices, warehouses, and industrial facilities throughout Bergen County, NJ. A clean exterior projects professionalism and brings in customers.

Exterior Window Cleaning

Streak-free exterior window cleaning removes dirt, hard water deposits, and grime from Bergen County homes and businesses for a spotless, professional finish.

Solar Panel Cleaning

Dirty panels lose efficiency. Our safe, low-pressure cleaning removes pollen, bird droppings, and grime to restore maximum energy output for Bergen County solar systems.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

A clogged dryer vent is a fire hazard. We remove lint, debris, and blockages from dryer vent systems in Bergen County homes — improving safety and dryer efficiency.

Awning Cleaning

Sun, rain, and biological growth take a toll. We safely clean fabric and metal awnings on Bergen County homes and storefronts — preserving appearance and extending lifespan.

Perimeter Downspout Jetting

High-pressure jetting clears stubborn downspout clogs and underground drain lines, preventing water backup and protecting Bergen County foundations from damage.

Christmas Light Installation

Skip the ladder. D&J handles design, installation, and post-season removal of Christmas lights for Bergen County homes and businesses — magic without the stress.

Snow Removal

Reliable snow removal for driveways, walkways, parking lots, and commercial properties throughout Bergen County, NJ — keeping you safe and accessible all winter.
