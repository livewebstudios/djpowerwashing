Power Washing Services Across Sussex County, NJ

D&J Power Washing brings professional residential exterior cleaning to Sussex County, NJ — lake homes, log cabins, large rural lots, and the small downtowns of Newton, Sparta, Vernon, and Hopatcong. Family-owned. Free estimates.

Exterior Cleaning Built for Sussex County, NJ

Sussex County is the most rural county in northern New Jersey — rolling farmland, lake communities (Lake Hopatcong, Lake Mohawk, Highland Lakes, Cranberry Lake), state parks, and small downtowns. The housing stock leans toward larger lots, log cabins, lake homes, cedar-shake colonials, and ranches with mature trees overhead. The exterior cleaning challenges are different from the urban counties: heavy organic growth on shaded surfaces, weathered wood that demands a true soft wash, and big decks that take real abuse from the weather.

D&J Power Washing serves Sussex County with the family-owned care you expect. We know how to clean a 20-year-old wood deck without lifting the grain. We know how to handle algae-streaked vinyl on a north-facing wall under heavy tree cover. We know lake-home decks need annual attention if you want them to last.

Sussex County is a longer drive for us than our home county, but we work it into our schedule for clients who want true craftsmanship. Call us, describe the property, and we'll schedule a free on-site estimate.

Our Power Washing Services Across Sussex County, NJ

Soft House Wash

Low-pressure soft washing safely removes algae, mildew, and oxidation from siding, cedar shake, and stone on Sussex County homes — the right method for the older lake homes and rural properties that fill the county.

Deck & Paver Cleaning

Lake-home decks see the heaviest weather exposure of any property type we handle. We restore weathered wood with the right pressure and the right brightener.

Driveway, Patio & Sidewalk Cleaning

High-pressure cleaning lifts ground-in dirt, algae, and oil from concrete, asphalt, and pavers across Sussex County properties.

Gutter Cleaning

Sussex County's heavy tree cover means heavy gutter loads. We clear leaves, pine needles, and seed pods and verify proper drainage.

Exterior Window Cleaning

Streak-free exterior window cleaning for Sussex County homes. Hard water and pollen come off cleanly.

Solar Panel Cleaning

Dirty panels lose efficiency. Our safe, low-pressure cleaning restores energy output for Sussex County solar arrays.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

A clogged dryer vent is a fire hazard — especially in older homes with longer vent runs. We clear lint and blockages.

Perimeter Downspout Jetting

High-pressure jetting clears stubborn downspout clogs and underground drain lines, protecting Sussex County foundations.

Commercial Building Wash

Storefronts and small commercial properties across Newton, Sparta, Hopatcong, and Sussex Borough — pressure washing tailored to your business.

Christmas Light Installation

Professional design, install, and removal of holiday lights for Sussex County homes.

Snow Removal

Reliable snow removal for driveways and walkways across Sussex County, NJ — where winter is the real deal.
