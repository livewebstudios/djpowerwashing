Power Washing Services Across Hudson County, NJ

D&J Power Washing brings professional residential and commercial exterior cleaning to all of Hudson County, NJ — from the brownstones of Hoboken and Jersey City to the storefronts of Bayonne, Union City, and Secaucus. Family-owned. Free estimates.

Exterior Cleaning Built for Hudson County, NJ

Hudson County is one of the most densely populated and architecturally rich counties in the United States. Brick rowhouses, brownstones, mid-rise condos, mixed-use storefronts, and converted industrial buildings sit shoulder-to-shoulder across Jersey City, Hoboken, Bayonne, Union City, West New York, and the surrounding municipalities. Every one of those surfaces takes a beating from Hudson County's heavy traffic, industrial pollution, salt air off the river, and dense urban grime.

D&J Power Washing covers all of Hudson County with the same family-owned care we've built our reputation on. We understand the challenges of working in tight urban environments — coordinating with co-op boards and property managers, scheduling around restaurant lunch rushes, navigating permit parking, and getting access to back lots and rear walls without disrupting tenants. We bring the right equipment and the right paperwork.

Whether you own a single-family home in Bayonne, a brownstone in Hoboken, a mixed-use building in Jersey City, or you manage a portfolio of properties across the Gold Coast, we can handle the work. Call us, describe the property, and we'll schedule a free on-site estimate.

Our Power Washing Services Across Hudson County, NJ

Soft House Wash

Low-pressure soft washing safely removes algae, mildew, and pollution film from stucco, brick, vinyl, and painted surfaces on Hudson County homes and rowhouses without damaging mortar or trim.

Driveway, Patio & Sidewalk Cleaning

High-pressure cleaning lifts oil, gum, and ground-in grime from concrete walkways, driveways, and entry steps across Hudson County's residential and commercial properties.

Deck & Paver Cleaning

Rooftop decks, brownstone backyards, and paver patios get the same precision cleaning here as in any suburb — followed by joint re-sanding for a finished result.

Gutter Cleaning

Multi-story Hudson County buildings need gutters that actually flow. We clear leaves, debris, and bird nests and verify proper drainage on homes and small commercial properties.

Commercial Building Wash

Storefronts, restaurants, offices, and warehouses across Jersey City, Hoboken, Secaucus, and Bayonne — we tailor pressure washing to projects of any scale, scheduled around your business hours.

Exterior Window Cleaning

Streak-free exterior window cleaning for Hudson County homes and storefronts. Hard water, pollution film, and grime come off cleanly.

Awning Cleaning

Restaurant and retail awnings define a storefront. We safely clean fabric and metal awnings on Hudson County businesses to preserve appearance and extend lifespan.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

A clogged dryer vent is a fire hazard — especially in multi-family Hudson County buildings. We clear lint and blockages and improve dryer efficiency.

Perimeter Downspout Jetting

High-pressure jetting clears stubborn downspout clogs and underground drain lines, preventing water backup against Hudson County foundations.

Christmas Light Installation

Professional design, installation, and removal of holiday lights for Hudson County homes and businesses. We handle the ladders so you don't have to.

Snow Removal

Reliable snow removal for driveways, walkways, and commercial parking lots across Hudson County, NJ.
