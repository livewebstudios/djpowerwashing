Our Power Washing Services in Fair Lawn, NJ

Soft House Wash

Low-pressure soft washing safely removes algae, mold, mildew, and oxidation from stucco, stone, vinyl, and painted siding on Fair Lawn homes. The right technique for any home exterior.

Driveway, Patio & Sidewalk Cleaning

High-powered pressure washing lifts oil stains, tire marks, algae, and ground-in grime from concrete, asphalt, and pavers across Fair Lawn properties. Your hardscape will look brand new.

Deck & Paver Cleaning

We restore weathered wood decks and tired brick pavers throughout Fair Lawn, removing algae, moss, and UV oxidation, then re-sanding paver joints for a polished finish.

Gutter Cleaning

Clogged gutters cause water damage, foundation problems, and erosion. We clear leaves and debris and restore proper drainage on Fair Lawn homes year-round.

Commercial Building Wash

Tailored pressure washing for retail storefronts, offices, warehouses, and mixed-use buildings throughout Fair Lawn, NJ. A clean exterior projects professionalism.

Exterior Window Cleaning

Streak-free exterior window cleaning removes dirt, hard water deposits, and grime from Fair Lawn homes and businesses for a spotless finish.

Solar Panel Cleaning

Dirty panels lose efficiency. Our safe, low-pressure cleaning restores maximum energy output for Fair Lawn solar systems.

Dryer Vent Cleaning

A clogged dryer vent is a fire hazard. We remove lint, debris, and blockages from dryer vents in Fair Lawn homes — improving safety and dryer efficiency.

Awning Cleaning

We safely clean fabric and metal awnings on Fair Lawn homes and storefronts — preserving appearance and extending lifespan.

Perimeter Downspout Jetting

High-pressure jetting clears stubborn downspout clogs and underground drain lines, protecting Fair Lawn foundations from water damage.

Christmas Light Installation

D&J handles design, installation, and post-season removal of Christmas lights for Fair Lawn homes and businesses.

Snow Removal

Reliable snow removal for driveways, walkways, parking lots, and commercial properties throughout Fair Lawn, NJ.
