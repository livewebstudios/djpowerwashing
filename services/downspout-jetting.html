Professional Perimeter Downspout Jetting in Bergen County, NJ

High-pressure jetting clears clogs and debris from your downspouts and underground drain lines — protecting your foundation, basement, and landscaping from water damage.

Expert Downspout Jetting for Homes & Businesses in Bergen County

Most homeowners think gutter cleaning solves their drainage problems. But the gutter is only half the story — the downspouts and underground drain lines that carry water away from your foundation collect their own buildup of leaf litter, sediment, and roots. When those lines clog, water backs up at the gutter line, overflows toward your siding, or pools at the foundation. The result: stained walls, basement seepage, and over time, real structural damage.

D&J Power Washing uses commercial-grade jetting equipment to push high-pressure water and a flexible nozzle through your downspouts and any connected drain lines. The water blasts out compacted debris, root intrusions, and sediment that a standard gutter clean simply can't reach. From century-old colonials in Hackensack to brand-new builds in Paramus to homes in Saddle Brook with mature trees overhead, perimeter jetting protects what your gutters alone can't.

Owners John and Danielle treat downspout jetting as foundation insurance — far cheaper than the basement waterproofing or grading work you'd otherwise need.

What to Expect from D&J's Downspout Jetting Process

01

Drain System Inspection

We trace your downspouts and underground drain runs, identify suspect clogs, and locate cleanout access points.

02

Surface Debris Clear

Loose leaves and accessible debris are cleared from the gutter and downspout opening before jetting begins.

03

High-Pressure Jet

A flexible jetter nozzle is fed through the downspout and drain line, blasting away compacted sediment, leaf packs, and root intrusions.

04

Flow Verification & Discharge Check

We confirm proper flow at the discharge point and visually verify your downspouts and drain lines are running clear before we leave.
