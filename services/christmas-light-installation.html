The DIY string lights from the big-box store look fine the first year. By year three, half the strands are out, the clips are warped, and you've spent more on replacements than a professional install would have cost.

D&J Power Washing offers full-service holiday light installation across Bergen County, NJ. What to Expect from D&J's Christmas Light Process

01

Design Consultation

We walk your property, talk through your vision and budget, and propose a layout — roofline, trees, walkways, columns, or signage.

02

Professional Installation

Commercial-grade LEDs are installed with proper clips and timer hookup. We secure every strand and test the full display before leaving.

03

In-Season Service

If a strand goes out or a bulb fails during the season, we come back and fix it — included in your install.

04

Takedown & Storage

After the holidays, we remove every clip and strand, and store your lights cleanly for next year. No tangled mess in your basement.
