Professional Deck & Paver Cleaning and Sanding in Bergen County, NJ

We revitalize weathered wood decks and tired brick pavers — removing algae, moss, UV oxidation, and organic growth, then re-sanding paver joints for a finished, polished result.

Expert Deck & Paver Cleaning for Homes & Businesses in Bergen County

Wood decks and paver patios are designed for outdoor living — but Bergen County's wet springs, humid summers, and freeze-thaw winters take a brutal toll on both. Wood greys out and grows black mildew streaks, while pavers lose their color, sprout weeds between the joints, and shift as the sand washes out. D&J Power Washing specializes in restoring both surfaces so they look (and function) like new again.

For wood decks, we use a controlled soft-wash approach combined with deck-safe cleaners that strip algae, mildew, and grey UV oxidation without splintering the wood or feathering the grain. For pavers, we apply the right pressure to remove organic growth from the surface and joints, then re-sand with polymeric joint sand that locks the pavers in place and resists weed growth for years. Homeowners in Saddle Brook, Paramus, and Fair Lawn consistently tell us this is the service that surprised them most — the difference is dramatic.

Owners John and Danielle personally inspect every deck and paver job. Wood and pavers are not surfaces you blast away at — they require the right product, the right pressure, and a finished hand-detail pass.

What to Expect from D&J's Deck & Paver Cleaning Process

01

Assessment & Plant Protection

We evaluate wood condition, paver joints, and surrounding plants — pre-rinsing landscaping before any cleaner touches the surface.

02

Surface-Safe Cleaning

Wood decks get a controlled soft-wash; pavers get appropriate pressure to lift moss, algae, and ground-in dirt without damaging the surface.

03

Polymeric Joint Sanding (Pavers)

After the surface dries, we sweep polymeric sand into all joints and activate it for a locked, weed-resistant finish.

04

Final Walkthrough

We rinse landscaping again, walk the work with you, and don't sign off until you're 100% happy with the result.
