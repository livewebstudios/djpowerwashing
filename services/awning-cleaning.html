Professional Awning Cleaning in Bergen County, NJ

Sun, rain, and biological growth take a toll on awnings. We safely clean fabric and metal awnings — removing stains, mold, and discoloration to preserve their appearance and extend their lifespan.

Expert Awning Cleaning for Homes & Businesses in Bergen County

An awning is one of the first things people notice about your property — a faded, mildew-streaked storefront awning tells customers your business is tired, and a stained residential awning ages your whole house. The catch is that awnings are notoriously easy to ruin: pressure-wash a fabric awning at the wrong setting and you'll force water through the seams, strip the protective coating, or destroy the stitching. Skip cleaning them altogether and the algae and mildew lock in until the fabric needs replacement.

D&J Power Washing has refined a soft-wash approach for awnings that does the job without the damage. We use awning-safe biodegradable cleaners worked in by hand or with soft brushes, then rinse with low-pressure water. This treatment lifts mildew, bird droppings, road grime, and oxidation from acrylic, vinyl, canvas, and metal awnings alike. From storefronts in Hackensack to retractable patio awnings in Saddle Brook to standing-seam metal canopies in Paramus, we get them looking sharp again.

Owners John and Danielle personally inspect every awning job. Done right, awning cleaning extends the life of the material by years — and saves you the cost of replacement.

What to Expect from D&J's Awning Cleaning Process

01

Material Inspection

We identify fabric type (acrylic, vinyl, canvas) or metal, check seams and stitching, and select the right approach for the surface.

02

Pre-Treat Stains

Bird droppings, mildew streaks, and heavy soiling are pre-treated with awning-safe cleaners before the main wash.

03

Soft Wash & Hand Detail

Cleaning solution is worked in by hand or with soft brushes, then dwelled briefly to break down organic growth.

04

Low-Pressure Rinse & Dry

A controlled, low-pressure rinse leaves the awning clean and intact. We confirm coverage from underneath as well.
