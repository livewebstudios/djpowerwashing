Professional Exterior Window Cleaning in Bergen County, NJ

Crystal-clear windows transform the look of any property. Our streak-free window cleaning service removes dirt, grime, water deposits, and buildup for a spotless, professional finish.

Expert Exterior Window Cleaning for Homes & Businesses in Bergen County

It's amazing how much dirty windows drag down the look of an otherwise well-maintained property. Pollen film, hard-water spots from sprinklers, road grime from passing traffic, and oxidation from screen mesh build up gradually until one day you realize you can barely see out. Worse, mineral deposits from sprinklers and rainwater runoff actually etch into glass over time if they're not removed regularly. D&J Power Washing handles exterior window cleaning the right way — leaving glass spotless, streak-free, and protected.

We use professional water-fed pole systems with purified water for upper-story windows, paired with traditional squeegee work on accessible glass. Purified water dries spot-free without any soap residue, which is why this method has become the standard for both residential and commercial window cleaning. From cape-style homes in Saddle Brook to two-story colonials in Paramus to retail storefronts in Hackensack, we get the same clean result every time.

Owners John and Danielle personally inspect every job. Frames, sills, and tracks get attention too — clean glass framed by a dirty sill is only half a job.

What to Expect from D&J's Exterior Window Cleaning Process

01

Walk & Count

We walk the property, count windows, identify any that need extra attention (sprinkler spots, sap, hard water), and confirm access.

02

Frame & Sill Prep

Frames, sills, and tracks are wiped or rinsed first so loosened grime doesn't run down onto freshly cleaned glass.

03

Purified-Water Wash

Water-fed pole with purified water on upper stories, traditional squeegee on lower glass — a streak-free, residue-free finish.

04

Final Walkthrough

We walk every window with you. Anything not perfect gets re-done before we leave.
