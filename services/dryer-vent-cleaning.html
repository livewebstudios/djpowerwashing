Professional Dryer Vent Cleaning in Bergen County, NJ

A clogged dryer vent is a leading cause of residential fires. We safely remove lint, debris, and blockages from your dryer vent system — improving drying efficiency, reducing energy costs, and protecting your home.

Expert Dryer Vent Cleaning for Homes & Businesses in Bergen County

Lint is one of the most flammable substances in your home — and dryer vents are designed to carry hot, lint-laden air outside through ductwork that can run anywhere from a few feet to thirty-plus feet through walls and ceilings. Over time, that ductwork builds up a coating of lint that restricts airflow, causes the dryer to run hotter and longer, and creates an active fire risk. The U.S. Fire Administration estimates roughly 2,900 home dryer fires every year, and the number-one cause is failure to clean.

D&J Power Washing performs full dryer vent cleaning the right way — disconnecting the dryer, accessing both the appliance side and the exterior termination, and using rotary brushes and high-CFM vacuums to fully clear the duct from end to end. We don't just hit the lint trap or the first few feet of duct. Whether your laundry is on the first floor of a ranch in Saddle Brook, the second floor of a colonial in Paramus, or in a multi-unit building in Hackensack, we have the equipment to reach every section.

Owners John and Danielle personally manage every job. We confirm proper airflow before and after, document what we removed, and check the exterior vent hood and damper for proper operation.

What to Expect from D&J's Dryer Vent Cleaning Process

01

Inspection & Airflow Check

We measure airflow at the exterior termination, identify duct routing, and check the lint screen and transition hose.

02

Disconnect & Access

The dryer is safely pulled out and disconnected so we can clean from both the appliance side and the outside vent.

03

Rotary Brush & Vacuum

Rotary brushes scrub the duct interior while a high-CFM vacuum captures every bit of lint — no mess in your laundry room.

04

Reconnect & Verify

Dryer is reconnected, exterior damper checked, airflow re-measured. We show you the difference before we leave.
