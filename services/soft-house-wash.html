Professional Soft House Wash in Bergen County, NJ

Low-pressure soft washing that strips years of algae, mildew, and oxidation from stucco, stone, and siding — without ever harming the surface underneath.

Expert Soft House Wash for Homes & Businesses in Bergen County

Your home's exterior is constantly under attack. Humidity, tree coverage, pollen, and the seasonal swings of the North Jersey climate all encourage algae, mold, and mildew to take hold of stucco, stone, vinyl, and painted siding. Once those organic stains take root, no amount of garden hose rinsing is going to bring back the original color. That's where a professional soft house wash from D&J Power Washing changes everything.

Soft washing is not pressure washing. We use specialized low-pressure equipment paired with professional-grade, biodegradable cleaning solutions that gently break down biological growth at its source. The result is a deeper, longer-lasting clean than blasting at high pressure ever could deliver — and zero risk of stripping paint, gouging stucco, or forcing water behind siding seams. From classic colonials in Saddle Brook to brick-and-stucco ranches in Paramus and Fair Lawn, soft washing is the right answer for almost every home exterior in Bergen County.

D&J Power Washing has refined this process over years of work in Rochelle Park, Hackensack, and the surrounding communities. Owners John and Danielle personally inspect every job, dial in the right cleaning mixture for the surface, and stand behind the result with our satisfaction guarantee.

What to Expect from D&J's Soft House Wash Process

  1. 01

    Property Walk & Assessment

    We inspect siding, identify problem areas, protect landscaping with pre-rinses, and confirm the right approach for your surface type.

  2. 02

    Soft Wash Application

    Our biodegradable solution is applied at low pressure, where it breaks down algae, mold, and mildew at the root.

  3. 03

    Gentle Rinse

    A controlled, low-pressure rinse lifts away dissolved grime, leaving a clean, even finish without any surface damage.

  4. 04

    Final Walkthrough

    We walk the property with you, address any spots, and rinse landscaping again. You don't sign off until you're 100% satisfied.
