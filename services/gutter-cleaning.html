Professional Gutter Cleaning in Bergen County, NJ

Clogged gutters cause water damage, foundation problems, and landscape erosion. We clear leaves, debris, and buildup from your gutters and downspouts to restore proper drainage and protect your property.

Expert Gutter Cleaning for Homes & Businesses in Bergen County

Bergen County is full of mature trees — and that means full gutters. Oak, maple, and pine debris accumulates fast in the spring and fall, and once a gutter clogs, water has nowhere to go but over the edge or back into your fascia, soffits, and foundation. By the time you see staining on the siding or pooling near the foundation, the damage has already started. Routine gutter cleaning is the cheapest preventative maintenance you can do for your home.

D&J Power Washing handles gutter cleaning the right way. We don't just blast leaves around with a leaf blower from the roof — we hand-clear debris, bag it for removal, flush the troughs and downspouts with water to verify free flow, and inspect the system for loose hangers, separated seams, or downspout problems. Whether you have a one-story ranch in Lodi, a two-story colonial in Saddle Brook, or a commercial property in Hackensack, our team is equipped to do the job safely and thoroughly.

Owners John and Danielle personally manage every job. Gutters are out of sight, but our reputation depends on doing them right.

What to Expect from D&J's Gutter Cleaning Process

01

Property Setup & Safety Check

We position ladders safely, set tarps to protect landscaping, and identify any roof or gutter conditions that need attention.

02

Hand-Clear All Debris

Leaves, twigs, and buildup are removed by hand from every section of gutter and bagged for clean disposal — no debris left behind.

03

Flush & Verify Drainage

We flush the entire gutter system with water to confirm proper flow and clear any partial downspout clogs.

04

System Inspection & Cleanup

Final visual inspection of hangers, seams, and downspouts. We clean up the ground and walk the property with you before leaving.
