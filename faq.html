Frequently Asked Questions — D&J Power Washing, Bergen County NJ

Real answers to the questions homeowners and businesses ask most. Don't see what you're looking for? Call (201) 917-8510 or send us a message — we're happy to help.

Section 1

About Our Services

What types of surfaces do you specialize in cleaning?
D&J Power Washing specializes in cleaning a wide range of exterior surfaces, including vinyl siding, stucco, stone, brick, wood decks, concrete driveways, patios, pavers, and commercial building facades. We adjust our techniques and equipment for each surface to ensure the best results without damage.
Do you offer any specialized services beyond standard pressure washing?
Absolutely. In addition to our standard residential services, we offer solar panel cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, perimeter downspout jetting, awning cleaning, exterior window cleaning, Christmas light installation, and commercial building washes. If your exterior needs attention, there's a good chance we handle it.
Can D&J handle both residential and commercial projects?
Yes. We serve both residential homeowners and commercial clients throughout Bergen County, NJ. Our team is experienced in scaling service to any size — from a single-family home in Saddle Brook to a multi-unit commercial property in Paramus or Hackensack.
How often should I schedule exterior cleaning for my home?
Most homes in the Bergen County area benefit from an annual or bi-annual cleaning. The North Jersey climate — with its humidity, tree coverage, and seasonal variation — promotes algae, mold, and mildew growth on exterior surfaces. Regular cleaning preserves your home's appearance and protects the underlying materials from long-term damage.
Section 2

Process & Procedures

How do I schedule an estimate with D&J?
Just reach out through our contact form or give us a call. We'll schedule a convenient time for a free on-site visit, assess your property, and provide a detailed written estimate — no obligation, no pressure.
How long does a typical power washing job take?
Project duration depends on the size of the property and the scope of work. A standard house wash typically takes 2–4 hours. Larger properties or multi-service jobs may take a full day. We'll give you a realistic time estimate during the quoting process so you can plan accordingly.
Are your cleaning products safe for the environment and my landscaping?
Yes. We prioritize eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning solutions that are effective on dirt and biological growth but safe for your lawn, garden, and landscaping. We take precautions to rinse and protect plant areas before and during the cleaning process.
What safety measures are in place during the cleaning process?
Safety is always a priority at D&J Power Washing. Our technicians are trained in proper handling and application techniques. We use industry-standard professional equipment, follow all safety protocols, and ensure the work area is secure throughout the job.
Section 3

Customer Satisfaction

What sets D&J Power Washing apart from other exterior cleaning companies?
A few things: we're family-owned, which means John and Danielle personally stand behind every job. We use eco-friendly solutions. We offer a full range of services under one roof — so you're not juggling multiple contractors. And we serve Bergen County because we live here — our reputation in this community matters to us personally.
Do you offer satisfaction guarantees?
Yes. If you're not satisfied with the results of our work, we'll come back and address it. We stand behind every job we complete.
How can I provide feedback or raise a concern after the job is done?
Just contact us — by phone or through our website. We take feedback seriously and we respond quickly. Our goal is to make sure every client is genuinely happy with the outcome.
Can I set up recurring maintenance service with D&J?
Absolutely. We offer recurring maintenance plans customized to your property and schedule — whether that's annual, bi-annual, or seasonal. Regular maintenance is the best way to keep your property looking great year-round while avoiding more intensive (and more expensive) deep cleans down the road.
